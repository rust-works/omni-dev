- **`chrono` replaced by `jiff`, clearing RUSTSEC-2026-0543** ([#2257](https://github.com/rust-works/omni-dev/issues/2257)): `chrono` is unmaintained with no patched release. Every timestamp omni-dev writes keeps its exact form (RFC 3339 in UTC with `Z` and 0, 3, 6 or 9 fraction digits; a commit's date in its author's offset), so persisted files, daemon replies and the request log read the same as before. `rmcp` is updated to 3.12.0 with its `chrono` feature turned off, so `chrono` is no longer in the dependency tree.
