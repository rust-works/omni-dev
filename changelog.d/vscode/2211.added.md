- **Reopen a recently closed worktree** ([#2211](https://github.com/rust-works/omni-dev/issues/2211)): the Worktrees view gains a collapsed **Recently Closed** section, last in the tree, listing worktrees whose window was closed or which were removed, newest first with their age (removed ones are marked and show a bin icon). Reopen one with the inline button or by double-clicking its row, or run **omni-dev: Reopen Closed Worktree…** from the command palette for a picker. A worktree whose folder is still on disk simply reopens its window. Recreating a *removed* worktree asks first, in a modal that names the branch, where it will be recreated and whether it comes from the branch or from its last recorded commit, and always warns that uncommitted changes are not recoverable; cancelling creates nothing. Needs a daemon of this release: an older daemon sends no list, so the section never appears and the command reports that the daemon cannot list recently closed worktrees.
