- **BREAKING — the hidden `gmail` and `drive` stubs are deleted** ([#2224](https://github.com/rust-works/omni-dev/issues/2224), [ADR-0095](docs/adrs/adr-0095.md)): the one-release stubs that v0.47.0 left behind after the move to [gwi](https://github.com/rust-works/gwi) ([#2203](https://github.com/rust-works/omni-dev/issues/2203)) are gone, so `omni-dev gmail ...` and `omni-dev drive ...` now fail with clap's unrecognised-subcommand error instead of a message pointing at gwi. Use `gwi gmail ...` and `gwi drive ...` (install with `cargo install --git https://github.com/rust-works/gwi`), `gwi import` to copy your accounts and settings from `~/.omni-dev`, and `gwi-mcp` for the MCP tools.
