- **The worktrees daemon keeps a recently-closed list, and `worktrees recent` / `worktrees reopen` use it** ([#2211](https://github.com/rust-works/omni-dev/issues/2211), [ADR-0096](docs/adrs/adr-0096.md)): closing a window, closing a repo's last window and removing a worktree with Close Worktree each add an entry (path, repo, branch, head commit, and whether the worktree was removed from disk), newest first, capped at 50 entries and 30 days and kept across a daemon restart in a `0600` `worktrees-closed.json`. A window reload, or reopening a folder by hand, leaves no entry. The list rides the `tree`/`subscribe` snapshot as an additive `recently_closed` field and the new `recent-closed` op. `worktrees reopen <PATH>` opens a closed window; a **removed** worktree is recreated only after the daemon's plan is shown and confirmed (`-y` skips the prompt, `--dry-run` stops at the plan), on its branch, or from its recorded commit if the branch was deleted. Uncommitted changes in a removed worktree cannot be recovered, and the plan says so. The `reopen` op accepts only a path the daemon recorded. The VS Code extension's Recently Closed group and command are in its own changelog. `worktrees ui` does not show the list yet.
